# Slide 1: Historical Growth - Netflix

## Key Financial Metrics

### Revenue Growth (CAGR)
- **2020-2024 Revenue CAGR**: ~12%
- **2020 Revenue**: $25.0 billion
- **2021 Revenue**: $29.7 billion
- **2022 Revenue**: $31.6 billion
- **2023 Revenue**: $33.7 billion
- **2024 Revenue**: ~$36.2 billion

### Net Income Growth (CAGR)
- **2020-2024 Net Income CAGR**: ~18%
- **2020 Net Income**: $2.8 billion
- **2021 Net Income**: $5.1 billion
- **2022 Net Income**: $4.5 billion
- **2023 Net Income**: $5.4 billion
- **2024 Net Income**: ~$7.0 billion

### Free Cash Flow (FCF) Growth (CAGR)
- **2020-2024 FCF CAGR**: ~45%
- **2020 FCF**: -$3.3 billion (negative)
- **2021 FCF**: -$0.2 billion (negative)
- **2022 FCF**: $1.6 billion
- **2023 FCF**: $6.9 billion
- **2024 FCF**: ~$6.5 billion

## Margin Analysis

### Gross Margin
- **2020**: 38.3%
- **2021**: 41.0%
- **2022**: 40.5%
- **2023**: 41.0%
- **2024**: ~43.0%
- **Trend**: Improving margins due to scale and content efficiency

### Operating Margin
- **2020**: 18.3%
- **2021**: 20.9%
- **2022**: 16.3%
- **2023**: 20.6%
- **2024**: ~23.0%
- **Trend**: Strong recovery and improvement

### FCF Margin
- **2020**: -13.2%
- **2021**: -0.7%
- **2022**: 5.1%
- **2023**: 20.5%
- **2024**: ~18.0%
- **Trend**: Dramatic transformation to cash generation

## Growth Analysis

### Subscriber Growth
- Transitioned from subscriber growth focus to revenue per user and profitability
- **2020**: 204 million subscribers
- **2024**: ~280 million subscribers (including paid sharing initiatives)

### Key Growth Drivers
1. **Paid Sharing**: Crackdown on password sharing added millions of paying subscribers
2. **Ad-Supported Tier**: Launched in late 2022, driving lower-priced subscriber growth
3. **Content Efficiency**: Better ROI on content spend
4. **International Expansion**: Continued growth in emerging markets
5. **Gaming**: New revenue stream with mobile gaming integration

### Revenue per Membership
- Increasing through price optimization and paid sharing
- Ad-tier creating new monetization opportunities
- Average revenue per membership growing 5-8% annually
