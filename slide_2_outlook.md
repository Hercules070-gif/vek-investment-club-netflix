# Slide 2: Outlook - Netflix

## Management Outlook for 2025-2026

### Near-Term Guidance (2025)
- **Revenue Growth**: Expected 10-13% year-over-year
- **Operating Margin**: Target of 24-26%
- **Free Cash Flow**: Expected $6-8 billion annually
- **Subscriber Growth**: Focus shifting from subscriber numbers to revenue and engagement metrics

### Management's Strategic Priorities

1. **Content Excellence**
   - $17+ billion annual content budget
   - Focus on hit-driven content with broad appeal
   - Increased local language content for regional markets
   - Balance between films, series, and live content

2. **Advertising Business**
   - Ad-tier reaching critical scale with 15-20 million subscribers
   - Goal: $1+ billion in ad revenue by 2025
   - Building out ad tech capabilities and measurement tools
   - Attracting premium advertisers

3. **Paid Sharing**
   - Continued rollout and optimization globally
   - Converting shared accounts to paying members
   - Expected to add millions of incremental subscribers

4. **Gaming and New Verticals**
   - Mobile gaming expansion
   - Potential for cloud gaming
   - Merchandise and live experiences

## Long-Term Outlook and Plan

### 5-Year Vision (2025-2030)

**Revenue Goals**
- Target: $50-60 billion in annual revenue by 2030
- Driven by subscriber growth, ARPU increases, and advertising

**Margin Expansion**
- Long-term operating margin target: 25-28%
- FCF margin: 20%+
- Improved content efficiency and scale benefits

**Global Reach**
- Target: 400+ million households globally
- Penetration in emerging markets (India, Southeast Asia, Africa)
- Continued dominance in existing markets

**Product Innovation**
- Live sports and events (potential)
- Interactive content expansion
- Enhanced personalization with AI
- Potential bundling strategies

## Analyst Expectations

### Wall Street Consensus (2025 Estimates)
- **Revenue**: $38-40 billion
- **EPS**: $18-20
- **Free Cash Flow**: $7-8 billion
- **Operating Margin**: 24-25%

### Growth Drivers According to Analysts
1. Password sharing crackdown revenue (+$3-4 billion potential)
2. Advertising scale reaching profitability
3. Price increases in mature markets
4. International market penetration
5. Gaming ecosystem development

### Key Metrics to Watch
- Engagement hours (time spent on platform)
- Revenue per membership growth
- Ad-tier subscriber adoption rate
- Churn rates by tier
- Content cost per hour of engagement

## Risks to Outlook

### Competition Risks
- Intensifying streaming competition (Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+)
- Traditional media companies building streaming platforms
- Free ad-supported streaming services (FAST channels)

### Market Saturation
- Mature market penetration limits in North America and Europe
- Slower subscriber growth rates
- Increased customer acquisition costs

### Content Risks
- Rising content costs
- Writer and actor strikes (labor disruptions)
- Hit-driven volatility
- Dependence on third-party content producers

### Economic Risks
- Consumer spending pressure during economic downturns
- Foreign exchange headwinds
- Inflation impacting production costs
- Potential recession reducing discretionary spending

### Regulatory Risks
- Content regulation in key markets
- Data privacy regulations
- Potential antitrust scrutiny
- Local content quotas

### Technological Risks
- Piracy and account sharing circumvention
- Changing consumer preferences
- New entertainment technologies disrupting streaming
