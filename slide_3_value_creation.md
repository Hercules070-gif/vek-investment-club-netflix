# Slide 3: Historic Value Creation - Netflix

## Stock Price Performance

### 1-Year Performance (2024-2025)
- **Starting Price (April 2024)**: ~$600
- **Ending Price (April 2025)**: ~$650-700
- **Return**: +8% to +17%
- **Performance**: Moderate growth, benefiting from strong Q4 2024 results and paid sharing success

### 5-Year Performance (2020-2025)
- **Starting Price (April 2020)**: ~$420
- **Ending Price (April 2025)**: ~$650-700
- **Total Return**: +55% to +67%
- **CAGR**: ~9% to 11%
- **Key Periods**:
  - 2020-2021: Strong pandemic rally (+50%)
  - 2022: Major correction (-50% from peak) due to subscriber losses
  - 2023-2025: Recovery phase (+70% from 2022 lows)

### 10-Year Performance (2015-2025)
- **Starting Price (April 2015)**: ~$60 (split-adjusted)
- **Ending Price (April 2025)**: ~$650-700
- **Total Return**: +1,000% to +1,100%
- **CAGR**: ~27% to 28%
- **Exceptional long-term performance** driven by streaming revolution

## Sector Comparison

### Streaming/Media Entertainment Peers (5-Year Returns)

**Netflix (NFLX)**
- 5-Year Return: +55% to +67%
- Volatility: High (β ≈ 1.3)

**Disney (DIS)**
- 5-Year Return: -15% to -5%
- Challenges: Streaming losses, linear TV decline
- Recent recovery with Disney+ profitability

**Warner Bros Discovery (WBD)**
- 5-Year Return: -60% to -50% (since merger)
- Struggles: Debt burden, streaming challenges

**Paramount (PARA)**
- 5-Year Return: -50% to -40%
- Issues: Transition challenges, heavy competition

**Comcast/NBCUniversal (CMCSA)**
- 5-Year Return: +10% to +20%
- Diversified business model provides stability

### Analysis
- Netflix has **significantly outperformed traditional media companies**
- Pure-play streaming model proved advantageous
- Early mover advantage in streaming maintained
- Stronger pricing power and content ROI than competitors

## Stock Performance Deep Dive

### Disappointing Periods and Explanations

**2022 Major Decline (-50% from peak)**

**Reasons:**
1. **Subscriber Losses**: First subscriber decline in Q1 2022 (-200k subscribers)
2. **Saturation Fears**: Market concerned about growth ceiling in mature markets
3. **Competition**: Disney+, HBO Max, Paramount+ intensifying competition
4. **Macro Concerns**: Rising interest rates hurt high-growth stocks
5. **Password Sharing**: Revenue leakage estimated at 100M+ households
6. **Content Spend**: Concern about unsustainable content spending (~$17B annually)

**Recovery Factors (2023-2025):**
1. Successful paid sharing rollout
2. Ad-tier launch and monetization
3. Return to subscriber growth
4. Margin expansion and FCF generation
5. Improved content efficiency

## Dividend Analysis

### Current Status
- **Netflix does NOT pay dividends**
- Company reinvests all earnings into content and growth
- No dividend history since going public

### Capital Allocation Philosophy
- Prioritize organic growth investments (content)
- Focus on increasing enterprise value
- Return cash through potential buybacks, not dividends

## Share Buyback Track Record

### Buyback History

**2021-2022**
- Limited buyback activity
- Focus on content investment

**2023-2024**
- **$5 billion authorization** announced
- Began systematic repurchases
- Bought back ~$2-3 billion in shares
- Average repurchase price: ~$350-450

**2025 Outlook**
- Continued buyback program
- Expected $2-3 billion annual repurchases
- Opportunistic approach based on stock price

### Impact
- Share count reduction: ~2-3% annually
- EPS accretion from reduced share count
- Signal of management confidence
- Capital return now that FCF is strongly positive

## Stock-Based Compensation (SBC)

### SBC Levels
- **Annual SBC**: $800 million to $1 billion
- **As % of Revenue**: ~2.5-3%
- **Impact**: Moderate dilution offset by buybacks

### SBC Strategy
- Used to attract and retain top talent
- Critical for technology and content talent competition
- Primarily equity grants to executives and engineers

### Net Dilution
- Gross dilution from SBC: ~2-3% annually
- Offset by buybacks: ~2-3% annually
- **Net effect**: Roughly neutral to slight dilution

## Value Creation Summary

### Strengths
✓ Exceptional 10-year returns (+1,000%)
✓ Strong recovery from 2022 lows
✓ Outperformance vs. traditional media peers
✓ Transition to FCF generation enabling buybacks
✓ Market leadership maintained

### Concerns
⚠ High volatility (β > 1.3)
⚠ No dividend for income investors
⚠ Moderate SBC dilution
⚠ Growth rate slowing from historical levels
⚠ Valuation dependent on execution of new initiatives

### Investor Takeaway
Netflix has created tremendous shareholder value over the long term, with the streaming model proving superior to traditional media. Recent focus on profitability and cash generation marks a maturation phase, with capital returns (buybacks) beginning but dividends unlikely near-term.
